Your job is LABELING, not judgment. Do not estimate market impact. Do not predict prices. Do not summarize.

For each post return:

{
  "post_id": string,               // copy exactly
  "topic": string,                 // ONE value from the TOPIC list below

  "target_sector": string[],       // ONLY values from the SECTOR list. [] if none.
  "mentioned_companies": string[], // US tickers of companies EXPLICITLY NAMED in the text. Do not infer. [] if none.
  "direction": "positive" | "negative" | "neutral",  // the post's stance toward its main target (a hostile post about China is "negative"; praising a company is "positive")
  "intensity": integer 1-10,       // POLICY CONCRETENESS scale below — not emotional tone
  "market_relevance": integer 0-3  // scale below
}

  Trade        tariffs, trade deals, trade deficits, import/export, USMCA

  Semiconductor, Technology, Financials, Energy, Healthcare, Industrials, Consumer, Autos, Defense, Materials, Real Estate, Utilities, Crypto, Bonds/Rates, Broad Market

INTENSITY (policy concreteness, 1-10):
  1-2  passing mention of the subject

  5-6  strong claim or threat without a concrete measure ("China will pay a huge price")
  7-8  policy warning or a concrete measure named ("considering tariffs on chips")

MARKET_RELEVANCE (0-3):

  2  could directly affect an industry or asset class, but target is unspecified ("tariffs are good", "rates are too high")
  3  tariffs / rates / sanctions / regulation / a named company with a specified target — immediate market relevance ("reviewing tariffs on Chinese chips", "Powell must cut now")

SUBTOPIC — use the SHORTEST canonical noun phrase and reuse it across posts. Do not append "Rules", "Standards",

Rules:
- Attacks on the Fed chair are topic "Fed", relevance >= 2, even if phrased as a personal insult.
- Praise or criticism of a named company is topic "Company", include its ticker, relevance >= 2.
- A company investment / new plant / onshoring announcement (a named company investing, building, hiring) is

  even if the post credits tariffs for it. Use topic "Trade" only when the post itself states a tariff or trade

- A post that is a news headline plus a URL (shared article, no original statement by the author): label the
  headline's subject, but intensity <= 6 — a shared headline is at most a strong claim, never the author's own
  announcement, even if the headline says the administration did something.
- If the post is about several things, label the dominant one.
- Be consistent: identical wording must produce identical labels.
